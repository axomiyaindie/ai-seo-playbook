# 01 · Foundations

## How AI answers are produced
1. **Training knowledge**: what the model learned from its training data (brand mentions, Wikipedia, forums, docs, news).
2. **Live retrieval (RAG)**: the AI searches the web (Google, Bing, its own index), fetches pages and summarises them with citations.

To win you need both: be **known** (training and entity presence) and be **retrievable and citable** (live search).

## The visibility stack
| Layer | Goal |
|---|---|
| Crawlability | Bots can fetch and render your pages |
| Indexability | Pages are in Google/Bing indexes (AI search relies on them) |
| Relevance | Pages answer real questions clearly |
| Authority | Others cite and mention you (links, reviews, press, communities) |
| Entity clarity | Models understand who and what you are |
| Citability | Content is easy to quote: facts, definitions, data, structure |

# Part B — Modern AI SEO

> Part of the [AI SEO Playbook](https://github.com/rakibuli10/ai-seo-playbook). Part A: [Traditional SEO](../traditional-seo/README.md) · Bridge: [How they connect](../how-they-connect.md)

AI search changes where visibility happens. People increasingly get answers from **AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot** — often without clicking a link. If an LLM doesn't know, trust, and cite your product, you're invisible to a growing share of your audience.

Part B covers the tactics that earn **retrieval, citation, and recommendation** in AI answers. It builds directly on Part A: every tactic here assumes your pages are already crawlable, indexable, well-structured, and genuinely helpful. **Do Part A first.**

## The core insight

**AI answers are a retrieval-and-citation problem, not a ranking problem — but they sit on top of the same index and ranking systems as traditional search.**

That has three consequences:

1. **Part A is the prerequisite.** If your page can't be crawled, indexed, and ranked in classic search, it won't be retrieved for AI answers.
2. **Retrieval is the battleground.** AI systems select a subset of indexed pages to ground their answers. Being *retrievable* — crawlable, well-structured, clearly about a topic — matters more than ever.
3. **Citation is the new click.** When an AI answer cites you, that's visibility even without a click. Structuring content so it's easy to quote — clear claims, sourced facts, scannable structure — pays off.

## What's different from traditional SEO

| Traditional SEO | Modern AI SEO |
|---|---|
| Optimise for rankings | Optimise for retrieval and citation |
| Think in pages | Think in passages and claims |
| Keywords | Entities and intent |
| Crawl budget | AI crawler management (training vs. retrieval bots) |
| Rich results (schema) | Structured data for machine readability |
| Rankings + clicks | Citations + AI referral traffic + brand mentions |
| Human audience | Human audience + AI systems that answer for humans |

None of the traditional work becomes obsolete. AI search **adds** a layer on top.

---

## The one-paragraph summary

AI answers retrieve from the same index as classic search, so **Part A is the foundation**. On top of it, Part B adds: managing AI crawlers (distinguishing training bots from retrieval bots), publishing `llms.txt` and structured data for machine readability, structuring content at the **passage level** so specific claims are easy to extract and cite, clarifying **entities** (consistent naming, `sameAs`, organisation schema), and **measuring** AI citations and referral traffic. The emerging **agentic layer** (MCP, A2A) extends discovery and transaction to AI agents. Every tactic assumes Part A is healthy.

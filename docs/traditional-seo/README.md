# Part A — Traditional SEO

> Part of the [AI SEO Playbook](https://github.com/rakibuli10/ai-seo-playbook). Part B: [Modern AI SEO](../modern-ai-seo/README.md) · Overview: [How they connect](../how-they-connect.md)

Traditional SEO is the foundation. Before AI Overviews, ChatGPT, or Perplexity existed, search engines were already crawling pages, building an index, and ranking results — and **AI answers sit on top of that same pipeline**. If your site can't be crawled, indexed, and ranked in classic search, it won't be retrieved for AI answers either.

This part covers the seven fundamentals every site needs. **Start at 01 and work through in order** — each chapter builds on the last.

## How to use this part

- **If you're new to SEO:** read 01 → 07 in order. Each builds on the last.
- **If you're experienced:** treat it as a reference. Chapter 04 (technical) and 07 (measurement) are the most commonly underinvested.
- **If you're auditing a site:** use chapter 07's measurement framework to diagnose, then jump to the chapter that addresses the failure (crawl → 04, content → 03/05, links → 06).

---

## The one-paragraph summary

Search is a **three-stage pipeline** (crawl → index → rank). You get into it by **matching query intent** (02) with a **well-structured page** (03) on **technically sound infrastructure** (04) that is **genuinely helpful** (05) and **backed by authority** (06) — then you **measure and iterate** (07). AI answers draw on the same pipeline, so this work is the prerequisite for AI visibility, not a substitute for it.

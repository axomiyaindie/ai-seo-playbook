## Why this exists

Search is no longer ten blue links. People now get answers from **AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini and Copilot**. If an LLM doesn't know, trust and cite your product, you are invisible.

This is a free, community-built playbook for **SEOs, marketers, founders and developers** who want their products and content to:

1. **Rank** on traditional search engines
2. **Appear** in AI Overviews and AI answers
3. **Get cited** by LLMs as a trusted source

## Terms in one minute

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Search Engine Optimization: ranking in classic search |
| **GEO** | Generative Engine Optimization: being used and cited in generative AI answers |
| **AEO** | Answer Engine Optimization: being the direct answer (featured snippets, voice, AI answers) |
| **LLMO** | Large Language Model Optimization: being known and recommended by models |

# AI SEO Launch Checklist

## Access
- [ ] Site indexable (no accidental `noindex`, canonicals correct)
- [ ] robots.txt allows search/citation bots you want
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- [ ] Key content visible in server-rendered HTML

## Entity
- [ ] Clear About, Pricing, Docs, Contact pages
- [ ] Organization + Product/SoftwareApplication JSON-LD
- [ ] Consistent brand description across LinkedIn, GitHub, Crunchbase, directories
- [ ] Listed on relevant review sites and directories (G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, etc.)

## Content
- [ ] Page for each core use case, comparison, alternative and FAQ
- [ ] Answer-first intros; tables; dates; named authors
- [ ] At least one piece of original data or research
- [ ] Visible "last updated" dates

## Authority
- [ ] Earned mentions: press, newsletters, podcasts, communities (genuine participation)
- [ ] Customer reviews on third-party sites

## Measure
- [ ] Prompt tracker set up and baseline recorded
- [ ] AI referral segment in analytics
- [ ] Monthly review and iteration
